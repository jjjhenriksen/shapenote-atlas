# Fa-template barline diagnosis: selective stem safeguard recovers topology, not quality

Date: 2026-10-15 America/Los_Angeles. Continues the
[saved-artifact survival trace](sh1991-fa-survival-20261015.md) on
[PR #475](https://github.com/jjjhenriksen/shapenote-atlas/pull/475), advancing
[#471](https://github.com/jjjhenriksen/shapenote-atlas/issues/471).

## Decision

**The first barline loss is now localized to REDUCTION, and a narrowly scoped
intervention reduces it. The revised recognizer still fails six quality gates;
do not promote it or evaluate its held-out arm.**

On the fixed five training scans, initial physical-bar removals fall from
**235 to 83**. Final bars rise **114 → 302**, measure stacks **18 → 64**, and
audited triangles with timed slots **13 → 44**. These are measured improvements
over rejected v1, not evidence of source-certified bars or correct scores.
Candidate pitch recall remains below the switch-off control, while precision,
fill discrimination, other-shape retention, duration and onset still fail.

## Earliest divergence and removal mechanism

The separate diagnostic engine records every physical bar's system, staff,
glyph, source-image bounding box, intrinsic grade and runtime ID at entry/exit
of every recognition step. Cross-run witnesses use source geometry plus glyph
provenance, not runtime IDs. Each removal records its call stack. A subsequent
probe records exclusion opponents, their best grades, and head support at the
instant of conflict resolution. Original logs remain local; the numeric report
includes SHA256 pointers, line provenance and compact inventory deltas.

**All five scans first diverge at REDUCTION/EXIT.** HEADS and STEMS preserve
identical physical-bar geometry in the two original arms. During REDUCTION,
`SigReducer.reduceFoundations()` calls `SIGraph.reduceExclusions()`, which
removes the lower-best-grade interpretation. All **235** on-arm bars removed
at this stage lose to overlapping **stems**, not directly to noteheads.
Of those stems, **152 have nonempty, exclusively fa-head support at decision
time; 83 have mixed fa/non-fa support**. This is runtime support evidence,
not a join inferred from the final graph after pruning.

Concrete continuation of the earlier Samaria witness: the original physical
bar on staff 2 near x=317 has box `(316,290,4,54)` and glyph 27. In the
same-binary guard-off probe, bar #28 has best grade **0.786741**, while its
overlapping stem has **0.973397** and eight logical fa heads. The resolver
deletes the bar. The revised rule deletes that fa-only stem instead. The bar
survives REDUCTION and the first-system column is present after MEASURES.
The report preserves the exact conflict log line and both geometries. IDs
are only within-run log locators, not cross-run identities.

This establishes the previously missing path: bar geometry survives detection
and stem construction, but competing head-supported stems win during conflict
reduction; partial-column cleanup then amplifies the loss. It does **not** prove
that every competing stem is a source error or that every original bar is valid.
The template matcher’s creation of these dense fa interpretations still needs
its own discrimination study; we have not isolated a specific template-scoring
or font-mask defect.

## One isolated intervention

The new default-off JVM property `atlas.faStemBarGuard` changes only the loser
of a **direct physical-barline/stem exclusion** when that stem currently has
one or more head–stem links and **all linked heads are logical fa shapes**.
In that case, discard the competing stem rather than the bar. Mixed-support
stems, unsupported stems, ordinary heads, direct fa-head/bar conflicts, all
other exclusions and grade ties retain upstream ordering.

No template, mask, anchor, fill rule, classifier, source pixel, measure-column
rule, rhythm algorithm, exporter or quality threshold changes. This is an
experimental priority policy, **not** a new confidence calibration or a rule
to freeze all old bars. It can preserve false bars and reject real fa stems;
independent source review is still required.

The design and original gates were frozen before enabling the rule. No
post-result parameter search or second active safeguard was run. The earlier
conflict-probe binary also contained an **unactivated** direct-fa-head policy;
its property was never enabled. It contributed diagnostic evidence only and
is not bundled with the active stem safeguard. Its separate patch and tests
are retained for exact provenance rather than silently omitted.

## Downstream recovery and residual shape damage

Reuse the frozen 210-head source audit, its two existing coordinate
adjudications and distance-only one-to-one matching at 0.5 interline. No new
labels are tuned to the result. These source observations are single-AI visual
annotations, not independent human ground truth.

| Audited source cohort | Switch off | Fa-v1 on | Revised |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Triangles retained, of 62 | 10 | 58 | 58 |
| Triangles with timed slots | 8 | 13 | 44 |
| Triangles with unique export layout witnesses | 8 | 13 | 43 |
| Correct fill among retained triangles | 1/10 | 43/58 | 43/58 |
| Other shapes retained, of 148 | 148 | 143 | 144 |
| Other shapes with timed slots | 142 | 17 | 125 |
| Other shapes with unique export witnesses | 141 | 16 | 98 |
| Other retained heads mislabeled fa | 0 | 40 | 40 |
| Unmatched graph candidates in audit interiors | 8 | 239 | 143 |

Thus **44 of 58 retained triangle locations now get timed slots**, but 14
still do not. Other-shape scheduling recovers substantially over v1, yet is
still worse than control. Other-shape fill correctness is **135/144** versus
**148/148** in control. Open-triangle fill remains **1/16** correct; the
intervention does not solve it. Unmatched detections are review workload, not
certified false positives.

Whole-image timed-head coverage is **687/735 (93.5%) → 399/1,851 (21.6%) →
1,045/1,480 (70.6%)**. Final raw heads and notes include false detections.
The revised **1,032** exported pitched notes are not proof of more correct
music: candidate-reference insertions rise from 2 to **210**. Unique layout
witnesses establish geometry/context survival, not correct pitch, fill,
duration or onset; unresolved joins must not be counted as proven export losses.

## Unchanged score gates

All five exports parse in each arm. Score agreement uses the same unreviewed
candidate XML: 965 pitched reference events and 1,001 timed reference events.
No source-certified accuracy claim follows from these comparisons.

| Candidate agreement | Switch off | Fa-v1 on | Revised |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Pitch recall | 57.9% | 16.3% | 55.6% |
| Pitch precision | 81.7% | 47.3% | 52.0% |
| Duration recall | 58.5% | 22.1% | 52.9% |
| Written measure/onset recall | 28.6% | 1.5% | 21.4% |

The revision passes only off-control event equivalence, triangle-retention
gain and export count. It fails triangle fill (74.1% < 80%), non-triangle
retention (97.3% < 98%), required pitch-recall gain (+2 pp over control),
precision loss limit (2 pp), duration loss limit (1 pp) and onset loss limit
(1 pp). **Six failures remain.** No gate is relaxed to recognize the topology
improvement. No engine is promoted, no model is fine-tuned, no held-out image
is recognized, and this does not close #471.

## Next evidence-backed experiment

Keep this result frozen. The remaining **83 initial removals** are exactly
the mixed-support class left untouched by the policy. Do **not** respond by
blanket-protecting all bars: inspect source-reviewed bar/stem/head crops and
the decision-time support lists first. Build the small training discrimination
benchmark spanning all four shapes, open/filled states and non-note marks,
including dense candidates at bar positions. Record competing template scores
and whether each head has independent image support. Then test one change to
false fa-candidate generation or support weighting, preserving the current
retention/precision/fill/duration/onset gates. This is a proposed next study,
not work already performed or a promise that it will pass.
