# V5 — force explicit internal-anchor comparisons

Overnight 2026-10-15/16, PR #475. **Read-only diagnostic, not musical acceptance.**
[Protocol and recipe](../../experiments/audiveris-anchor-v5/README.md),
[numeric evidence](sh1991-anchor-v5-20261016.json).

A new pinned engine makes **90,720** evaluations at all **210** frozen
positive source-head points. It records actual internal pivots, template
origins, slim centers/sizes, mask errors, ignored pixels and hole white ratios.
Each of four filled/open template pairs uses three anchors, both
source-center and common-internal-anchor placement, and a fixed 3x3 pixel grid.
No proposal is created. No source label is supplied to the engine.

**All 10 same-binary training page runs export.** Probe-off and probe-on arms
preserve frozen V3 candidate written events, measure counts, head audit and
candidate-reference metrics. The instrumentation therefore does not itself
repair recognition. Separate engines and historical artifacts remain intact.

At the fixed zero-offset source center / MIDDLE_LEFT anchor:

| Source stratum | Template pair | Filled wins | Open wins |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Open triangles (18) | Fa left | 11 | 7 |
| Open triangles (18) | Fa up-right | 6 | 12 |
| Filled triangles (44) | Fa left | 25 | 19 |
| Filled triangles (44) | Fa up-right | 24 | 20 |
| Open squares (12) | Generic BLACK/VOID | 5 | 7 |
| Filled diamonds (8) | Diamond | 8 | 0 |

These are **paired template scores**, not final recognition accuracy. The
opposite triangle orientation can have a better hollow score for irrelevant
background, so taking the most favorable family would be misleading.
Several correct-orientation filled scores still beat hollow at labeled open
sources; hole samples exist and many pixels are neutralized. This does not
establish a single mask bug or justify per-source threshold tuning. Source
squares are controls scored by generic templates, not native square labels.

The historical V3 gates remain unchanged and failing. The evidence supports
an isolated next test: reuse the existing generic black-to-void hole rule
with native same-orientation Fa partners, the exact chosen internal anchor
and the existing threshold. That intervention is separately frozen and
measured in [V6](sh1991-native-hole-v6-20261016.md).

same-binary equivalence. Full logs, pixels and engines stay local/ignored.
No held-out run, source certification, default enablement or promotion.
