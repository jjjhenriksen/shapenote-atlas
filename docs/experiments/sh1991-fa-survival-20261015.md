# Fa-v1 survival trace: measure topology and rhythm are the bottleneck

Date: 2026-10-15 America/Los_Angeles. Follow-up to the
[rejected fa-template prototype](sh1991-fa-template-v1-20261015.md), advancing
[#471](https://github.com/jjjhenriksen/shapenote-atlas/issues/471).

## Finding

**The added heads are not simply failing to attach to stems or chords.** In
both arms, every retained head has a head–stem relation, belongs to a
stem-linked head chord, and is listed in a measure. The large loss appears
between measure membership and usable rhythm scheduling. Simultaneously,
measure topology collapses: **89 saved stacks become 18**, and the logs record
**46 deleted partial barline columns instead of 1**.

This changes the recommended next target: investigate barline survival and
cross-staff measure consistency before assuming missing stem attachment is
the principal defect. Existing links can still be *wrong*; presence is not
musical correctness.

This analysis uses only the **ten saved training runs**. It does not modify
the recognizer, run recognition, change labels, or access held-out scans.
V1 remains rejected; no improvement to transcription has been demonstrated.

| Unique graph↔export layout witnesses | 667 | 164 |

Timed-slot coverage falls from **93.5% to 21.6%**. A saved measure can list a
chord even when rhythm reconstruction never assigns it a usable starting slot.
The exporter iterates voice `BEGIN` entries at stack slots, not every head
listed in a measure. This explains why many more saved heads need not produce
more exported notes.

Attachment presence is not benign recognition: multi-head chords increase
from **13 to 291**, with **138 on-arm chords containing four or more heads**
(none in the control). This is consistent with over-detection inflating chord
clusters; it is not a source-reviewed judgment of every cluster.

The export count is a separate population, not a proven head-by-head join.
Only mutually unique layout witnesses are accepted. Duplicate heads at the
same export position, missing geometry, or multiple possible export notes
remain unresolved. In particular, **235 timed graph heads without a unique
export witness are not 235 proven export deletions**: the on-arm export also
contains 168 timed pitched notes without a unique graph witness. Layout
ambiguity must not be mislabeled as an export failure.

## Following actual source-observed heads

The frozen 210-head audit is more informative than the raw detection totals.
Reuse its annotations, two prior coordinate adjudications, and distance-only,
one-to-one matching within 0.5 interline identically in both arms. Shape/fill
does not influence matching. Source annotations are single-AI observations,
not independent human ground truth.

| Source-observed cohort | Observed | Retained off → on | Timed-slot assignment off → on | Unique export witness off → on |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Triangles | 62 | 10 → 58 | 8 → 13 | 8 → 13 |
| Other shapes | 148 | 148 → 143 | 142 → 17 | 141 → 16 |
| Total | 210 | 158 → 201 | 150 → 30 | 149 → 29 |

Thus **45 of the 58 recovered triangle locations have no timed-slot
assignment**. Triangle retention improves, but most recovered heads never
reach the exporter's ordinary timed-note path. Meanwhile, previously retained
non-triangle source notes lose rhythm scheduling on a much larger scale.
An export witness demonstrates presence at the corresponding layout location,
not correct source pitch, fill, duration, onset, or measure alignment.

## The barline mechanism that the logs actually establish

| Topology evidence | Switch off | Switch on |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Physical barline nodes | 402 | 114 |
| Logical staff-barline nodes | 382 | 94 |
| Saved measure stacks, including cautionary | 89 | 18 |
| Partial columns deleted as minority | 0 | 27 |
| Partial columns deleted at half coverage | 1 | 19 |
| Partial columns expanded by majority | 15 | 6 |

The pinned `MeasuresBuilder.enforceSystemConsistency()` checks barline
coverage across the system's non-tablature staves. A partial column represented
on fewer than half the staves is deleted; exactly half is also deleted; a
majority is expanded. `Column.delete()` removes its remaining physical bars.
The on-arm logs explicitly record these decisions **during MEASURES, before
CHORDS and RHYTHMS**.

Concrete witness: Samaria system 1, original barline column near x=317, is
retained in the off graph on all four staves. In the on-arm log, line 62
reports a partial column with only staff 1 (`id=6`) and staff 3 (`id=50`);
line 63 records **deleting half column**. The saved on graph consequently has
no bar at that column, and its entire first system is one measure stack rather
than eight. Similar logged deletions occur throughout the probe.

This proves one *amplifying mechanism*: partial cross-staff bar coverage causes
the remaining bars in a column to be discarded, merging measure regions.
**It does not prove why the other staff bars first disappeared.** The runs
retain final graphs, not HEADS/STEMS/REDUCTION snapshots. Head/bar/stem
competition and reduction are hypotheses to inspect, not a resolved causal
claim. The figures are engine-detected topology, not a new source-certified
barline annotation.

## Residual export failures are real, but not new to this patch

The on-arm Samaria and Vale of Sorrow logs also show `PartBarline` reload and
`PartwiseBuilder` measure-visit failures. The **same warning families already
exist in their off controls**. They occur at different compressed measure
labels after topology collapse, so they cannot honestly be presented as a new
fa-specific exporter defect.

Vale of Sorrow is the largest remaining population discrepancy: 106 heads
have scheduled slots but only 43 timed pitched notes are exported. Export
visits for P4 measures 2 and 3 fail. Ortonville, Bellevue, and The Church's
Desolation have equal scheduled-head and exported-pitch counts in the on arm,
although individual layout witnesses are often ambiguous. Count equality
does not certify individual survival or score correctness.

## Next bounded experiment — proposed, not run

1. Keep v1 and these results frozen. Add diagnostic snapshots/counts at
   HEADS, STEMS, REDUCTION, and entry/exit of MEASURES in a separate version,
   identifying physical barlines by source geometry/glyph provenance rather
   than generated IDs alone. This should locate the *first* bar losses.
2. Test a single topology safeguard against the same fixed training control:
   isolate how enabling fa recognition affects established bar candidates and
   the partial-column deletion rule. Do not globally freeze all detected bars
   or treat every old bar as ground truth. The original control itself already
   misses/merges source measures.
3. Preserve all prior fill/other-shape/pitch/precision/duration/onset gates.
   Require independently reviewed barline evidence before calling retained
   topology correct. Only consider held-out evaluation if the training gates
   pass; do not combine this with several template/fill/anchor changes.

This is more targeted than a broad fine-tune or a speculative stem-anchor
change. It remains a hypothesis to test, not a promised repair.

## Replay and verification

[Machine-readable trace](sh1991-fa-survival-20261015.json) contains per-image
graph/export/log/run SHA256 receipts, original log line numbers and column
actions, code-evidence hashes/symbols, stage counts, and per-source-head
attribution. Restricted source bytes and raw engine artifacts stay in ignored
`work/`.

```sh
python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_sh1991_*.py'
python3 scripts/sh1991_fa_survival.py --output /tmp/fa-survival-replay.json
```

The script verifies source/label/input/output hashes and rescored agreement
with the original ten receipts, rejects cohort/split/switch/engine/receipt
drift, and checks graph/export/log/run bytes again before writing its report.
It invokes no recognizer. Unique export attribution uses single-staff
`P1..Pn` mapping, measure **labels** (not array offsets), voice, staff, and
the exporter's rounded `default-x` geometry within one tenth. It never uses
pitch or source shape to force a match. Ambiguities remain explicit.

Clone-safe tests exercise attachments without rhythm, missing/negative slot
times, nonsequential measure labels, dangling relations, ambiguous/nonreused
export witnesses, excluded grace/rest events, cautionary regions, unsupported
part mapping, and log-line provenance. These checks validate the analysis,
not the recognizer or musical correctness.

Verification at this checkpoint: **32 Python tests passed**, all **60
inventoried saved experiment files remained byte-identical**, and an
analysis-only replay reproduced the JSON exactly. No engine build or
recognition process was launched. The original v1 numeric report and all its
run receipts remain unchanged.
